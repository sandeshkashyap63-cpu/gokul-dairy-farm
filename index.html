About Us

A name dairy farmers across India trust

Established in 2001 in Kunjpura, Karnal — the dairy heartland of Haryana — Gokul Dairy Farm is a leading breeder and supplier of premium milch cattle. We are proud producers and traders of high-quality cows and buffaloes including HF, Murrah Buffalo, Sahiwal, Tharparkar, Gir and Jersey.

Every animal at our farm is raised under expert supervision, kept in clean and spacious surroundings, fed well, and regularly vaccinated by experienced veterinary doctors. Our promise is simple: healthy cattle, honest dealing, and fair prices — whether you need one cow or a bulk order.

  • Run by Mr. Ravi with a dedicated team of cattle experts
  • Located beside Shri Krishan Gopal Gaushala, Kunjpura, Karnal
  • Cattle supplied to dairy farms across India

Our Roots

Rooted in Kunjpura, the dairy heart of Haryana

Since 2001, Gokul Dairy Farm has grown on the fertile soil of Karnal — raising indigenous and high-yield breeds the way nature intended: on open land, clean feed and genuine, everyday care.

Every cow and buffalo we raise carries the trust of a family that has lived and breathed dairy farming for over two decades. No middlemen, no shortcuts — just healthy cattle, reared right and sold with honesty.

Before the milk, there is the farm — and the family that tends it.
